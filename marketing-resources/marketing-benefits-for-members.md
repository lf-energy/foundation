# Marketing Benefits for LF Energy Members

## Marketing Engagement Philosophy

LF Energy provides focused marketing programs designed to increase the awareness, adoption, and impact of its open source projects. As a vendor-neutral foundation, all marketing activities center on open source projects, standards, and community outcomes in line with [LF Energy’s brand guidelines](https://lfenergy.org/about/brand-guidelines-for-lf-energy-and-hosted-projects/).

Member organizations gain visibility in the context of:

* Their contributions to LF Energy projects.  
* Collaborations with other members.  
* Real-world deployment or use of LF Energy technologies.

This approach strengthens the overall ecosystem, benefiting members through increased adoption, interoperability, and accelerated innovation in the energy sector.

## How Members Interact with Marketing

Members primarily engage with LF Energy marketing through their involvement in hosted projects and the broader community. Marketing activities are designed to amplify these contributions and connect them to broader industry narratives.  
Key Participation Areas:

* Project Contribution: Contributing to and maintaining LF Energy projects.  
* Use Cases: Sharing real-world deployments and adoption stories.  
* Collaboration: Working with other members on joint initiatives.  
* Thought Leadership: Providing subject matter expertise through contributed content or research.  
* Events: Participating in LF Energy-hosted events, webinars, and working groups.

## Core Marketing Benefits

All LF Energy members receive a set of core marketing opportunities tied to their participation:

* Member Announcements: Inclusion in LF Energy membership announcements, often coordinated as part of broader momentum releases.  
* Platform Visibility: Company logo placement on LF Energy web properties following membership announcement.  
* Social Media & Newsletter: Opportunities for member mentions on LF Energy social channels and potential inclusion in the LF Energy newsletter, linked to project activity or ecosystem developments. Review the [LF Energy social media guidelines](https://lfenergy.org/about/brand-guidelines-for-lf-energy-and-hosted-projects/social-media-guidelines-for-projects-sigs/).  
* Event Access: Opportunities to participate in LF Energy-hosted webinars, meetups, and events. Review the [webinar and meetup guidelines](https://lfenergy.org/about/guidelines-for-meeting-and-webinar-tools/).  
* Marketing Coordination: Access to the LF Energy marketing team for guidance on aligning announcements and activities with the broader ecosystem.

## Expanded Marketing Opportunities

As members deepen their involvement in LF Energy projects, additional opportunities emerge that highlight real-world impact and collaboration.  
Opportunities for Deeper Project Engagement:

* Case Studies and Adoption Stories: Showcasing how LF Energy projects are used in real-world deployments.  
* Contributed Content: Sharing insights, technical perspectives, and lessons learned through LF Energy blog and content channels.  
* Media Engagement: Inclusion in selected announcements related to project milestones, collaborations, or industry impact.  
* Speaking Opportunities: Opportunities to represent projects and share expertise at industry events.  
* Joint Exhibits: LF Energy can coordinate shared exhibition spaces at industry conferences, lowering the cost of presence for multiple members.  
* Research: Participation in collaborative white papers and research initiatives.

## Marketing Advisory Committee (MAC)

The Marketing Advisory Committee (MAC) is the primary forum for LF Energy members to help shape the foundation’s marketing and outreach strategy. It serves as a bridge between members, projects, and the LF Energy marketing team.

### Role and Responsibilities

The MAC is responsible for advising on and supporting the design and execution of LF Energy’s outreach and marketing efforts, ensuring activities reflect the needs and priorities of the ecosystem.

### How to Engage via the MAC

Through participation, members can:

* Contribute ideas for marketing campaigns, messaging, and industry positioning.  
* Provide input on events, webinars, and outreach initiatives.  
* Help identify opportunities to highlight project progress, adoption, and impact.  
* Coordinate with other members and projects on joint storytelling.

### Participation and Meetings

* Strategic Members may appoint a voting representative.  
* General Members may appoint a representative to participate and contribute.  
* Meetings are held on the Fourth Thursday of each month from 7:00 – 8:00 AM US Pacific Time.  
* To update or appoint a representative, contact pr@lfenergy.org.   
* Review [past meeting agendas and minutes](https://lf-energy.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/HOME/pages/13671274/Marketing+Advisory+Committee+MAC).

## Getting Started

Members interested in engaging with LF Energy marketing are encouraged to take the following steps:

1. Participate in hosted projects and working groups.  
2. Share relevant updates, use cases, and milestones.  
3. Coordinate announcements with LF Energy marketing.  
4. Join the Marketing Advisory Committee (MAC).

For all marketing-related inquiries, contact pr@lfenergy.org.
