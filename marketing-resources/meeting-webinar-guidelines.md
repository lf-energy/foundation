# LF Energy Meeting and Webinar Guidelines

LF Energy provides our projects with several options for scheduling meetings and webinars, whether virtual or in-person. The choice of meeting tool depends on the purpose of the meeting and the audience. This document outlines the guidelines for when each tool should be utilized.

## Committee/Working Group Meetings

For committee meetings, whether private, such as a TSC meeting to discuss a security vulnerability, or public, such as a monthly working group meeting, the [LFX Project Control Center](https://projectadmin.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/) should be used. Project maintainers should have access to the tool; if anyone does not have access and needs it, please file a [support ticket](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/issues/new/choose).

Instructions on how to create a meeting in PCC can be found [here](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/project-control-center/v2-latest-version/collaborations/meetings).  
If a meeting is recorded, the recording will be available a few hours following the meeting’s completion. The system will also automatically track who attended the meeting and for how long so you can review this information later.

## Webinars and Meetups

For webinars and meetups (in-person or virtual) about LF Energy projects, whether public or invite-only, you should use the [OCGroups platform](https://ocgroups.dev/). The Linux Foundation provides project communities with access to this tool at no cost. See [Community-Organized Events](#community-organized-events) below for the event formats available and how to promote your event. There are two options for setting up a webinar or meetup:

1. LF Energy managed – in this case, you need to provide details about the webinar or meetup to the LF Energy communications team using our [marketing request form](http://marketingrequest.lfenergy.org/). Please be sure to fill out the form in as much detail as possible. Our team will then reach out with any questions and to confirm details, then will build out a registration page and virtual event platform (if needed). This is recommended for one-off activities.  
2. Self-serve – in this case, we will help you set up a login to the OCGroups platform to self-manage webinars or meetups. We strongly recommend reaching out to the communications team (pr@lfenergy.org) and booking a walkthrough of the platform. You will be able to schedule your own webinars and meetups as often as you like. You can also access analytics about how many folks registered and attended, as well as see registration lists. Note you will not have access to contact information such as email addresses for registrants. You can send emails through the platform to all registrants if you need to communicate anything or to send a post-event wrap up. The one requirement here is that if you are using a virtual event platform, it CANNOT collect any personally identifiable information such as phone numbers, email addresses, etc. due to privacy regulations (the Zoom dial-in that OCGroups can create automatically is fine to use). We recommend a tool that does not require a log in or pre-registration. If you need LF Energy to set up the virtual event platform for you, use our [marketing request form](http://marketingrequest.lfenergy.org/). Self-serve is recommended if your project plans to have multiple webinars or meetups on an ongoing basis.

## Community-Organized Events

This section covers events, meetups, workshops and similar gatherings that are organized by a project community and focused on a specific LF Energy project, whether in person or virtual. Committee and working group meetings, ad hoc meetings and company-run events are covered in the other sections of this document.

All community-organized events, public or invite-only, are managed through [OCGroups](https://ocgroups.dev/), using either the LF Energy managed or the self-serve option described in the Webinars and Meetups section above.

### Event formats

When you set up an event in OCGroups, you choose one of three formats.

|  | Public | Public, request to attend | Invite-only |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Who can see the event | Anyone | Anyone | Only people you invite |
| Who can attend | Anyone who registers | Requesters you approve | Only invited people |
| LF Energy promotion | Yes (see [Promotion](#promotion)) | Yes (see [Promotion](#promotion)) | Only if you request it |

**Public events** are fully open: anyone can find the event and register.

**Public events with request to attend** are listed and promoted publicly, but attendance is by approval. Interested people submit a request through the event page, and you review the requests and choose who to accept. Use this format when you want wide visibility but need to be selective about who attends, for example when space is limited or the audience is targeted.

**Invite-only events** are not publicly listed or promoted. You have two optional ways to widen or manage attendance:

* **Open later:** the event stays invite-only for a set period, then becomes public if space remains.
* **Request an invite:** interested people ask for an invite, and you choose which requests to accept.

### In-person, hybrid and virtual events

OCGroups supports in-person, virtual and hybrid events. For virtual and hybrid events, the platform can create a Zoom dial-in automatically, which can host up to 100 virtual attendees. If you expect more than 100 virtual attendees, this can be accommodated but requires additional steps, so please contact [pr@lfenergy.org](mailto:pr@lfenergy.org) for assistance.

### Getting started

Please coordinate with LF Energy to confirm the timing and scope of your event. If you are organizing in Europe, contact Stephan Gross, Director of Community ([sgross@linuxfoundation.org](mailto:sgross@linuxfoundation.org)). In other geographies, contact [pr@lfenergy.org](mailto:pr@lfenergy.org). The team will help with the access you need to the OCGroups platform so you can set up your registration system.

### Timing

We recommend at least 4 weeks' notice for any publicly promoted event, including request-to-attend events, so there is time to promote it and for people to plan. In-person events benefit from longer notice (8 weeks or more is ideal), especially when attendees need to arrange travel.

### Promotion

For public events, including public events where attendance is by request, LF Energy will:

* List the event on the [LF Energy events page](https://lfenergy.org/events/)
* Promote it on LinkedIn
* Include it in the monthly newsletter
* Share it with the Marketing Advisory Committee (MAC)
* Share an event marketing kit with the MAC and with you, so you can spread the word to your own contacts

For invite-only events, LF Energy does not promote the event unless you ask us to. You can request any of the options above, including sharing with the MAC and the marketing kit.

To request promotional help, file a ticket at the [marketing request form](https://marketingrequest.lfenergy.org) or email [pr@lfenergy.org](mailto:pr@lfenergy.org). Please allow the 4-week minimum so the listing, LinkedIn post and newsletter mention can be scheduled. The newsletter is monthly, so your event will be included in the issue that goes out before the event takes place.

#### Event marketing kit

The kit is self-serve. Complete the [Community Event Marketing Kit Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HM8Jw8zKhiH5euEaogIzS5Dv-tmgN4DzXOXFaJgaKGc/edit?usp=sharing) with your event details, social posts, newsletter blurb, email copy and graphics, and send it to [pr@lfenergy.org](mailto:pr@lfenergy.org). LF Energy will share the finished kit with the MAC and with you, so you can use it to promote the event to your own contacts and networks.

### Organizer checklist

Here is what we ask of organizers for a publicly promoted event. LF Energy handles the events page listing, LinkedIn posts, newsletter mention and MAC outreach on its side.

| When | What to do |
| ----- | ----- |
| 4 weeks before (8 weeks or more for in-person events) | Make sure your event is live on OCGroups, then submit the marketing request and your completed marketing kit |
| 3 to 4 weeks before (6 to 8 weeks for longer-notice events) | Share the event with your own networks and contacts using the kit |
| 1 week before | Email registrants through OCGroups with event details |
| After the event | Send a wrap-up email to registrants through OCGroups |

## Ad Hoc Meetings

If your project community needs to have an ad hoc meeting, perhaps with only core maintainers or one-on-one between two developers, you are welcome to use any tool you like. There is no requirement for these meetings to run through LF Energy tools. If you do wish to use one of these tools, use the [LFX Project Control Center](https://projectadmin.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/) (see the Committee/Working Group Meetings section above for instructions).

## Individual Organization Run Webinars/Meetups

If your organization wishes to host a webinar or meetup related to an LF Energy project, but with only your company organizing rather than the broader community, there are two options:

1. The first option is to run this using the same process outlined above in the Webinars and Meetups section. You still have the option for LF Energy to manage this or to do so under the self-service model, however, our team will need to review the topic and confirm it is relevant to the community. Note that under this option, you will not receive any personally identifiable information about registrants or attendees.  
2. If you are hosting this for business purposes such as generating sales leads or any other reason that requires the collection of personally identifiable information, reach out to us at pr@lfenergy.org to discuss the process and guidelines.

If you have any questions about the guidelines outlined above, contact pr@lfenergy.org for assistance.
