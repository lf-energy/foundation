This folder contains resources for the LF Energy community to assist with marketing efforts. Files include:

[Brand Guidelines](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/blob/main/marketing-resources/brand-guidelines.md): Guidelines around how to mention or discuss LF Energy and its hosted projects

[Meeting and Webinar Guidelines](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/blob/main/marketing-resources/meeting-webinar-guidelines.md): Explains how to organize and run in-person and virtual meetings where LF Energy or its projects are the primary focus.

[Social Media Guidelines](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/blob/main/marketing-resources/social-media-guidelines.md): Explains how to use social media to promote LF Energy projects, and how to set up and manage social media channels linked to specific projects.

[Marketing Support for LF Energy Hosted Projects](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/blob/main/marketing-resources/marketing-support-for-projects.md): Outlines the marketing services available to project communities, and how to take advantage of them.

[Marketing Benefits for LF Energy Members](https://github.com/lf-energy/foundation/blob/main/marketing-resources/marketing-benefits-for-members.md): Outlines the marketing services provided exclusively to LF Energy members, and how to utilize them.
